## 1. Situation Analysis

### Business Overview
nonairesumes.com occupies a uniquely-timed niche: the backlash against AI-written resumes is growing as hiring managers and ATS systems become better at detecting AI-generated content. The brand name itself is the SEO signal — it directly targets a search intent that barely existed three years ago and is now mainstream.

**Core differentiators:**
- Explicitly "Non AI" (brand + keyword alignment)
- Certified professional resume writers
- ATS optimization focus
- 60-day interview guarantee (3x more interviews or rewrite/refund)
- One-on-one consultation with matched writer
- Services: resumes, resume review, LinkedIn optimization, cover letters

**Location signals:** Tasmania, Australia (+61 phone), but content indicates global/English-speaking market targeting.

### Current Organic Visibility Gaps (inferred)
- Limited schema markup detected
- Blog content present but thin (2–3 posts indexed)
- No strong topical authority cluster established
- Brand searches likely dominate over non-branded traffic
- Competitor differentiation pages not prominent

| ResumeGo | Human writers, quality | No explicit anti-AI angle |
| Let's Eat Grandma | High quality, human | Premium price, US-focused |
| Resumeble | Volume + speed | AI-assisted, less differentiated |

### Competitive Advantage Positioning
nonairesumes.com has a first-mover advantage in the **anti-AI resume niche**. Most competitors either:
- Use AI and don't advertise it
- Use AI and advertise it as a feature
- Are silent on the topic

This creates a content moat opportunity: own all informational intent around "AI resumes" and capture users who distrust AI at the moment of decision.

### Content Gap Opportunities
- No competitor has a definitive "Can ATS detect AI resumes?" guide
- No competitor ranks for "human written resume vs AI" comparison content
- Thin coverage of "AI resume red flags recruiters notice"
- Underserved: industry-specific resume writing (tech, healthcare, finance, government)
