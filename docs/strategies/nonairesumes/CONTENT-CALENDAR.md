## Content Production Notes

### Format Standards
- All long-form content: 1 primary keyword + 3–5 LSI keywords
- Headers: H1 (one per page) → H2 (main sections) → H3 (subsections)
- Minimum 3 internal links per post (to service pages + related blog posts)
- Minimum 1 external link to authoritative source (LinkedIn research, BLS data, etc.)
- Always include CTA section at the end + 1 mid-content CTA

### E-E-A-T Signals Per Post
- Author byline with credentials (e.g., "Written by [Name], CPRW")
- "Last updated" date visible
- At least 1 specific statistic with source
- Real-world example or case study where possible

### AI Search Optimization Per Post
- Include a "Quick Answer" box at top (2–3 sentence direct answer)
- Use H2s phrased as questions where natural
- Include a bullet-point "Key Takeaways" section
- Add FAQ section at bottom of pillar pages (3–5 Q&As)
